POLICIES / PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

Your professional information deserves thoughtful handling.

This notice explains how Tech Talent USA handles information submitted through this website. It is a plain-language overview and should be reviewed against actual practices and applicable law before public launch.

Information we collect

When you submit a candidate profile, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number, years of experience, current location, key skills, additional details, and an optional resume in PDF or DOCX format.

How we use information

We use the details you provide to review your professional background, understand your career interests, contact you about potentially relevant opportunities, and respond to your questions or requests.

Storage and service providers

Profile information and uploaded resumes are stored electronically in systems used to operate this website and manage candidate enquiries. Email service providers may process notification details so our team can receive a submission. Access should be limited to people who need the information for recruitment-related work.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell candidate profile information or publish your resume on this website. Information may be processed by hosting, database, and email service providers that support the website, or disclosed where required by law. Obtain your permission before sharing your profile directly with a prospective employer or other recruiting party.

Retention and your choices

We retain submitted information only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, subject to applicable legal requirements. You may ask us to access, correct, or delete information you submitted by contacting us. We may need to verify your request.

Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the website, but no online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not submit sensitive personal information that is not needed to describe your professional background.

Contact

Important: This notice is not legal advice. Confirm that these statements match your real data practices, retention schedule, sharing process, and applicable privacy obligations before relying on it as a formal policy.
