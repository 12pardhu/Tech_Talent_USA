Privacy Policy

When you submit the candidate registration form, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, email address, phone number, WhatsApp number, years of experience, current location, key skills, additional comments, and an uploaded resume in PDF or DOCX format.

We use submitted information to review your professional profile, understand your experience and career interests, and contact you about relevant opportunities or follow-up questions.

Submitted profile information and uploaded resumes are stored electronically and are accessible to authorized personnel for recruitment-related purposes. Please submit only information you are comfortable sharing for these purposes.
