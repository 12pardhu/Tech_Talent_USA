04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
Start here.

A few helpful details about sharing your profile and what happens next.

Who can submit a profile?

Technology professionals interested in exploring career opportunities in the United States can share their experience and skills.

Do I need to upload my resume?

No. Resume upload is optional. You can submit your profile with your contact details and key skills, and share a PDF or DOCX resume later if you prefer.

What happens after I submit my profile?

Our team reviews the information you share. We may contact you if we have a relevant opportunity. Submission does not guarantee an interview, placement, or job offer.

Can I update my details later?

Yes. Contact us by email or phone and tell us which information you would like to update.

How is my information used?

Your details are used to review your professional background and contact you about relevant opportunities. Read our Privacy Notice.
